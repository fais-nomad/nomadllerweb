Health & Fitness

  • Physical Condition: Requires excellent cardiovascular fitness and stamina.
  • Prior Training: Pre-expedition hiking and endurance training is strongly advised.
  • Altitude Risks: Trekking above 4,000m involves inherent risks of Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).

Cancellation Policy

  • No refund for initial booking amount.
  • > 30 days prior: 50% of cost charged
  • 15–30 days prior: 75% of cost charged
  • 0–15 days prior: 100% of cost charged
  • Rescheduling: Allowed > 1 month prior (10,000 INR fee)

Insurance

Travel insurance is recommended. Insurance should include:

  • Medical coverage
  • Personal accident coverage
  • Air ambulance
  • Helicopter rescue services

Important Notes

  • Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months.
  • Changes: Route may change due to weather or safety.
  • Luggage: Strictly max 15kg per person for porters.
  • Cash: ATMs are unavailable at high altitude; carry NPR.

Terms & Conditions

  • Booking Confirmation: Confirmed upon receipt of deposit.
  • Force Majeure: Not liable for changes caused by natural disasters or unrest.
  • Medical Emergencies: Evacuation costs are borne directly by the client.
  • Jurisdiction: Legal matters subject to local jurisdiction.

Risk & Liabilities

  • Inherent Risks: High-altitude trekking involves altitude sickness, unpredictable weather, and rugged terrain.
  • Liability Release: Participants must sign an assumption of risk and release form prior to departure.
  • Personal Responsibility: Guests must follow the guide's safety instructions at all times.

Things to Remember

  • Acclimatization: Walk slowly ("Bistari, Bistari") and stay extremely hydrated to prevent altitude sickness.
  • Layering: Temperatures fluctuate drastically from hot valleys to freezing passes. Dress in removable layers.
  • Respect Culture: Always walk to the left of Mani walls and prayer wheels. Ask permission before photographing locals.
  • Leave No Trace: Carry a reusable water bottle and bring all plastic wrappers back to Kathmandu.
