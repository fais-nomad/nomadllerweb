RISHIKESH TO GHANGARIA

The Journey Begins

Days 01 - 03
01

Rishikesh to Govindghat

Our representative will meet you in Rishikesh, and you will start your journey towards Govindghat. The journey will take 9 to 10 hours, during which you can enjoy the beautiful landscapes. You will reach Govindghat where you will have dinner and rest.

🚌 Tempo Traveller
⏱️ 9-10 hours
02

Govindghat to Pulna & Trek to Ghangharia

After breakfast, we drive to Pulna. From there, we start our trek towards Ghangharia via Pulna village. On the way, enjoy beautiful mountain views. Reach Ghangharia in the evening for dinner.

🥾 9 km trek
🏔️ Elev: 9,800 ft
03

Trek to Valley of Flowers & Back

Explore the beautiful valley and see stunning views of the colourful exotic flowers. After spending time in the valley, we trek back to Ghangaria. Don't forget to capture the moments.

🥾 8 km trek
🏔️ Elev: 11,500 ft
Nomadller Luxury Expeditions Page 03

The High Pilgrimage

Days 04 - 06
04

Ghangaria to Hemkund Sahib & Back

A steep, challenging climb passing through dense forests and water streams. Soak in the beauty of the place, sip on piping hot tea served at the Gurudwara, and visit Laxman Mandir.

🥾 11 km trek
🏔️ Elev: 14,100 ft
05

Trek Ghangaria to Govindghat

Start the trek back towards Govindghat. From Govindghat, we will drive to Pipalkoti. On the way, cross sulphur water pools and breathe fresh air. Head back to your lodge by evening.

🥾 9 km trek
⏱️ 6-7 hours
06

Joshimath to Rishikesh

Wake up early morning to begin your journey back. The drive takes around 8-9 hours, and you will be dropped off at Rishikesh.

🚌 Tempo Traveller
⏱️ 8-9 hours
