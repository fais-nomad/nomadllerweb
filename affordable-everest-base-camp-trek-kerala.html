Understanding EBC Trekking

What is the Everest Base Camp Trek?

The Everest Base Camp (EBC) trek is one of the most famous trekking routes in the world. Reaching an altitude of 5,364 meters, it takes you through the heart of the Khumbu region, passing ancient monasteries, suspension bridges, and towering Himalayan peaks. For anyone passionate about trekking, standing at the base of the world's highest mountain is an unmatched achievement.

Is Trekking to EBC Difficult?

While trekking to Everest Base Camp requires physical fitness and stamina, it is not a technical climb. You do not need ropes or ice axes. As the leading trekking company in Kerala, we prepare our trekkers with comprehensive pre-trek fitness plans and gear guides to ensure a successful summit to Kala Patthar and Base Camp.
