DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1: Arrival in Kathmandu & Transfer to Hotel.
DAY 2: Kathmandu to Pokhara via scenic drive.
DAY 3: Pokhara to Ghandruk Village (Jeep Drive).
DAY 4: Trek to Chhomrong (Views of Annapurna South).
DAY 5: Trek to Dovan through dense forests.
DAY 6: Trek to Deurali (Bamboo & Rhododendron).
DAY 7: Reach ABC (4,130 m) via MBC. Spectacular views.
DAY 8: ABC to Bamboo (Descent through alpine forests).
DAY 9: Bamboo to Pokhara via Jhinu Hot Springs.
DAY 10: Return to Kathmandu (Tourist Bus).
DAY 11: Departure from Kathmandu Airport.
