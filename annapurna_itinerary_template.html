🏔️ Annapurna Circuit Expedition

14-Day Adventure • Nomadller Adventures

Expedition Timeline

DAY 01 Arrival in Kathmandu
Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, you will be transferred to your hotel. Spend the day exploring the vibrant city, preparing for your Annapurna Circuit Trek. Overnight stay at a hotel.
🛣️ Airport to hotel (5-7 km) ⏱️ 30 minutes
DAY 02 Kathmandu to Besi Sahar
After breakfast, start your journey with a scenic drive from Kathmandu to Besi Sahar. The drive offers beautiful views of terraced fields, rivers, and villages. This is where your trekking adventure officially begins! Overnight stay at a lodge.
🛣️ 173 km ⏱️ 6-7 hours
DAY 03 Besi Sahar to Upper Pisang
From Besi Sahar, take a thrilling 4WD Jeep ride to Pisang. You'll travel through rough, rugged terrain as the scenery transforms into lush forests and snow-capped mountains. This marks the start of the Annapurna Circuit trek in the higher altitude regions. Overnight stay at a lodge in Pisang.
🛣️ 70 km ⏱️ 6-7 hours
DAY 04 Pisang to Manang
Begin your trek from Pisang to Manang, a beautiful village nestled in the Annapurna Conservation Area. The trail offers stunning views of Annapurna II, III, and IV, as well as Gangapurna. The route passes through small villages like Bhraka, rich in Tibetan culture. Overnight stay at a lodge.
🥾 15 km ⏱️ 6-7 hours
DAY 05 Manang Acclimatization
Spend the day in Manang for essential acclimatization. Options:

Hike to Gangapurna Lake (3,540m): A short hike to the beautiful glacial-fed turquoise lake.

Optional Hike to Ice Lake (4,600m): A challenging trek up to Ice Lake (Kicho Tal) offering breathtaking panoramic vistas of Annapurna III, Annapurna IV, Gangapurna, and Tilicho Peak.
🥾 Gangapurna: 3km (1-2h) | Ice Lake: 10km (6-7h)
DAY 06 Manang to Shree Kharka
Today’s trek takes you from Manang to Shree Kharka, a small settlement on the way to Tilicho Lake. You will experience dramatic landscape changes, from lush green forests to barren, rocky trails. The views of Tilicho Peak and the Annapurna Range are breathtaking. Overnight stay at a lodge.
🥾 8-10 km ⏱️ 4-5 hours
DAY 07 Shree Kharka to Tilicho Base Camp
Trek from Shree Kharka to Tilicho Base Camp, the starting point for visiting Tilicho Lake. This part of the trail can be challenging, with narrow paths along the mountainside, but the stunning views make it all worthwhile. Overnight stay at a lodge.
🥾 6-7 km ⏱️ 4-5 hours
DAY 08 Tilicho Base Camp to Tilicho Lake
Begin an early morning trek to Tilicho Lake, one of the highest lakes in the world, situated at an altitude of 4,920 meters. After spending time at the lake, return to Shree Kharka for an overnight stay. The journey offers panoramic views of Tilicho Peak and surrounding mountains.
🥾 14-15 km ⏱️ 8-9 hours
DAY 09 Shree Kharka to Letdar
From Shree Kharka, trek to Letdar, a key stop leading to the Thorong La Pass. The trek will take you through alpine meadows and past yak pastures, with stunning views of the Annapurna range in the background. Overnight stay at a lodge.
🥾 10-11 km ⏱️ 5-6 hours
DAY 10 Letdar to Thorong Phedi / High Camp
Option 1 (Thorong Phedi - 4,420m): Ascend through rugged terrain and alpine meadows with views of Gungang Himal. Overnight at lodge.

Option 2 (High Camp - 4,850m): For well-acclimatized trekkers, continue past Phedi to High Camp. A steeper, demanding climb but provides a head start for the pass crossing. Overnight at lodge.
🥾 Phedi: 6-7 km (3-4h) | High Camp: 8 km (4-5h)
DAY 11 Thorong La Pass to Muktinath
This is the most challenging yet rewarding day of the trek. Start early to cross the Thorong-La Pass (5,416m), the highest point of the trek. The pass offers spectacular views of the Dhaulagiri and Annapurna ranges. After crossing the pass, descend to Muktinath, a sacred pilgrimage site. Overnight stay at a lodge.
🥾 16-18 km ⏱️ 8-10 hours
DAY 12 Muktinath to Pokhara
Travel by comfortable sofa bus from the high-altitude spiritual hub of Muktinath down to the lakeside city of Pokhara. Enjoy the transition from arid desert landscapes to lush subtropical greenery. Overnight stay in Pokhara.
DAY 13 Pokhara to Kathmandu
Board a sofa bus for the scenic drive back to Kathmandu, following the winding Trishuli River and terraced farmlands. Spend your last night in Nepal enjoying the vibrant energy of Thamel. Overnight stay in Kathmandu.
DAY 14 Departure
After breakfast, you will be transferred to Tribhuvan International Airport for your onward flight, concluding an unforgettable Himalayan adventure.

Trekking in the Himalayas involves inherent risks including altitude sickness and unpredictable weather. We require all participants to be in excellent physical condition. Cardiovascular training is recommended at least 2 months prior to the trek. Please consult your physician before undertaking high-altitude expeditions.
